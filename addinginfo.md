Professional Profile

Lawrence Phuka Chikapa, known online as Lawreay, is a Malawian software developer and systems builder based in Ntcheu, Malawi.

His work focuses on building practical software systems for education, administration, business operations, identity management, communication, and community-focused digital services.

Rather than concentrating on a single technology stack, his projects span PHP, Python, HTML/CSS, JavaScript/TypeScript, databases, APIs, automation, AI-assisted development, and information-management systems.

A recurring theme across his work is turning real-world administrative problems into lightweight digital systems that can be deployed and used by organisations with limited resources.

He is particularly interested in building technology that is useful within the Malawian context, including solutions for schools, universities, training centres, businesses, developers, and community organisations.
